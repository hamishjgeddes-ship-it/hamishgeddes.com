About me

Builder.
By nature.

I'm Hamish Geddes — a builder at heart. I've always been drawn to making things: understanding how they work, breaking them apart, and putting them back together better than before.

My story

My background spans technology, design, and product — but the through-line is always the same: I want to build things that are genuinely useful and well-crafted. I believe that good design and good engineering are the same thing, just viewed from different angles.

I'm based in New Zealand and work across a range of projects — some in public, some still in the garage. I'm energised by early-stage ideas and the messy, exciting process of turning them into something real.

Things I believe
Ship beats perfect The best way to learn is to make something real and put it in front of people.
Taste is a skill You develop it by paying close attention to things that are done well.
Simple is harder Complexity is easy. Simplicity takes real work — and is almost always better.
Execution is the thing Most ideas aren't original. What you do with them is what matters.
